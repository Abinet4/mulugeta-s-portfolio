Featured project

Betoch Marketplace

A multi-vendor furniture marketplace for Addis Ababa where customers compare and buy furniture from verified local sellers.

  • Laravel REST API with Sanctum auth and a React + Vite frontend, with separate workflows for customers, sellers and admins.
  • Seller onboarding: sellers apply, admins approve, and approved sellers manage their own listings and stock.
  • Checkout that splits each cart into one order per seller, reserves stock and supports cash on delivery.
  • Chapa online payments, with refunds and seller payouts tracked by admins.
  • Commissions, seller balances, settlements and sales reports with CSV export.
  • Email and SMS alerts, two-step sign-in by text, reviews, compare and quick view.
